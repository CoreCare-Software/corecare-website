# Test checklist — CoreCare Platform 1.4.2

## Deployment
- Confirm the displayed version is 1.4.2.
- Confirm there are no red error notifications.

## Save ticket
1. Open Tickets.
2. Open an existing ticket.
3. Change priority, status, assignee or category.
4. Press Save ticket.
5. Confirm the button briefly shows Saving….
6. Confirm the ticket detail window closes.
7. Confirm the Tickets queue remains visible.
8. Confirm the updated values appear in the queue.
9. Reopen the ticket and confirm the changes persisted.

## Status shortcuts
- Open an active ticket and press Mark resolved.
- Confirm the detail window closes and the ticket appears as resolved.
- Reopen that ticket and press Reopen ticket.
- Confirm the detail window closes and the ticket returns to in progress.

## Important distinction
- Confirm pressing Save ticket does not set the ticket status to Closed unless Closed was selected.
- Confirm pressing the × button closes the screen without saving unsaved edits.

## Regression
- Create a new ticket.
- Add a customer reply and an internal note.
- Log time.
- Confirm search and filters still work.
- Open product and organisation monitoring pages once.
